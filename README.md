## Project Corpus and Research Question
Corpus: The Decameron 
Research Question: How does The Decameron address and discuss the Black Death and what patterns in the explanations for the plague can be found throughout this document. Further, how might these explanations and patterns relate back to relgion during the 14th century and possibly what impact did these views have on the interactions between the church and society following the plague.
